Do’s
1)Think before you post – Make sure your content is respectful and appropriate.
2)Protect your privacy – Use strong passwords and adjust privacy settings.
3)Verify information – Check facts before sharing news or posts.
4)Be respectful – Communicate politely and avoid online arguments.
5)Use social media productively – Follow educational pages, career tips, and useful content.
Don’ts
1)Don’t overshare personal information – Avoid posting sensitive details like address or phone number.
2)Don’t spread fake news – Never forward unverified information.
3)Don’t engage in cyberbullying – Avoid negative comments, trolling, or harassment.
4)Don’t waste excessive time – Limit screen time to stay focused on studies.
5)Don’t post inappropriate content – Avoid anything that can harm your reputation or future career.
