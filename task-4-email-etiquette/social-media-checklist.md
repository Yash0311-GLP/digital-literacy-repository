Do’s
Think before you post – Make sure your content is respectful and appropriate.
Protect your privacy – Use strong passwords and adjust privacy settings.
Verify information – Check facts before sharing news or posts.
Be respectful – Communicate politely and avoid online arguments.
Use social media productively – Follow educational pages, career tips, and useful content.
Don’ts
Don’t overshare personal information – Avoid posting sensitive details like address or phone number.
Don’t spread fake news – Never forward unverified information.
Don’t engage in cyberbullying – Avoid negative comments, trolling, or harassment.
Don’t waste excessive time – Limit screen time to stay focused on studies.
Don’t post inappropriate content – Avoid anything that can harm your reputation or future career.
